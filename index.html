Our Story

Two Decades of
Coir Excellence

Disala Coir Industries is a Sri Lanka-based supplier with over 20 years of hands-on experience in the coir industry. We specialise as a primary local supplier of coco peat, and act as secondary suppliers of coir and fiber — working closely with processors and exporters to ensure product quality and reliable supply chains.

Beyond processed coir, we also deal in raw coconut husks, connecting local harvest with domestic and international buyers who seek sustainable, organic agricultural inputs. Our mission is simple: to connect the right buyer with the right product, reliably and transparently.

What We Supply

Our Product
Range

All products are natural by-products of the coconut palm — sustainably sourced from Sri Lanka's agricultural heartland and made available in bulk to buyers worldwide.

🌱
Primary Product Core

Coco Peat

Our flagship product. Coco peat is the natural spongy fiber extracted from coconut husks, prized globally as a superior growing medium and soil conditioner.

  • High water retention capacity
  • pH balanced (5.5–6.5)
  • Excellent aeration properties
  • Ideal for horticulture & hydroponics
  • Available loose — direct from machinery
  • Small-scale operation; bales not yet available
🪢
Secondary Product Available

Coir Fiber

Strong, durable natural fiber extracted from the outer husk of the coconut. Used in erosion control, geotextiles, mats, ropes, and handicrafts worldwide.

  • White & brown fiber grades
  • High tensile strength
  • Rot-resistant & biodegradable
  • Used in erosion control nets
  • Small-scale supply; bales not yet available
🌿
Secondary Product Available

Coco Pith

Also known as coir dust or coir compost. Coco pith is a fine, spongy material that serves as a sustainable peat moss alternative in soil-less agriculture.

  • Rich in lignin & cellulose
  • Ideal for mushroom cultivation
  • Organic soil amendment
  • Weed-free growing medium
  • Available washed & buffered
🥥
Also Available

Coconut Husks

Raw coconut husks — the outer shell of the coconut — are available for buyers who require unprocessed material for further coir processing or specialized uses.

  • Used in orchid & plant growing
  • Raw input for coir processing
  • Charcoal & fuel applications
  • Available in large quantities
  • Locally sourced, freshly harvested
